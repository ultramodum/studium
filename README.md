# Studium
This repository contains selected research papers, reports and utilities I've produced throughout my studies. They were handpicked to reflect my strongest academic interests: penetration testing, malware analysis and digital forensics. All work presented here was developed during my time at the _Norwegian University of Science & Technology (NTNU)_ and the _Swiss Federal Institute of Technology (ETHZ)_.
